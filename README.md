Analyses of questionnaire data collected in the Avian Contact Study, an online survey to gather data on human contact with birds and knowledge of avian influenza to understand avian influenza transmission.

The R script file 'vaccinationFV.R' contains code for analyses underpinning the manuscript titled 'Seasonal influenza vaccination in people who have contact with birds' by Amy Thomas, Suzanne Gokool, Harry Whitlow, Genevieve Clapp, Peter Moore, Richard Puleston, Louise E Smith, Riinu Pae, Ellen Brooks-Pollock
